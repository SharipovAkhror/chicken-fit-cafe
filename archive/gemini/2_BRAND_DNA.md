## 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING & ARCHETYPE

### 1.1 Brand Archetype
* **Primary Archetype:** *The Caring Master (Caregiver / Everyday Hero)*
* **Brand Persona:** An expert culinary master who genuinely cares for the health, energy, and time of every guest. Not a strict fitness trainer, but a warm, skilled craftsman offering honest, nourishing meals.
* **Core Value Proposition:** High-energy, honest, zero-heavy-oil baked chicken meals served within 15 minutes. Eliminates post-meal food coma while keeping guests 100% full.

### 1.2 Cultural & Regional Context
* **Location Focus:** Airport District & New Residential Complexes, Samarkand, Uzbekistan.
* **Cultural Synergy:** Merging traditional Uzbek hospitality (*Oshiq / Baraka*) with high-tempo, modern Fast-Casual standards.
* **Stereotype Neutralization:** Replaces the stereotype that "diet food is bland and dry" with juicy baked marinades, yogurt-herb sauces, and rich grain/vegetable bowls.

## 2. COLOR ARCHITECTURE & TOKEN MATRIX

All colors are mathematically selected to balance appetite stimulation, urban speed, and eco-freshness. The primary brand accent strictly incorporates the proprietary sequence `017` within its HEX value.

| Token Name | HEX Code | RGB Value | Primary Role & Visual Psychology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Accent** | `#FF5017` | `rgb(255, 80, 23)` | **Juicy Flame:** Energy, oven heat, baked crust. Primary CTA, signage, logo. |
| **Secondary Accent** | `#E2B024` | `rgb(226, 176, 36)` | **Saffron Gold:** Speed, richness, sauce accents, pricing badges. |
| **Eco Trust** | `#1E4D38` | `rgb(30, 77, 56)` | **Forest Leaf:** Freshness, green herbs, eco-responsibility, trust. |
| **Base Background** | `#F7F6F0` | `rgb(247, 246, 240)` | **Soft Canvas:** Warm air, clean plates, background canvas. |
| **Dark Neutral** | `#242526` | `rgb(36, 37, 38)` | **Charcoal Black:** UI grids, main body text, high contrast headers. |

### 2.1 Usage Ratios (60-30-10 Rule)
* **60% Base Canvas (`#F7F6F0`):** Provides high visual clarity, open breathing room, and premium airiness.
* **30% Dark Neutral (`#242526`) & Forest Leaf (`#1E4D38`):** Text hierarchy, interior seating, structural accents.
* **10% Primary (`#FF5017`) & Secondary (`#E2B024`):** High-priority Call-to-Actions (CTA), outdoor signage, price badges, seals.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM ("URBAN DRIVE")

The typographic pairing combines technological speed with warm, humanistic readability.

| Element | Font Family | Weight | Tracking / Leading | Character Style & Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display / Headlines** | `Unbounded` | Bold / ExtraBold | Tracking: -1.0% | Geometric, dynamic, high-impact velocity |
| **Subtitles / Badges** | `Unbounded` | SemiBold | Tracking: 0.0% | Uppercase, compact, navigation headers |
| **Body Text / Copy** | `Manrope` | Regular / Medium | Line Height: 150% | Open, warm, humanistic, zero eye strain |
| **Micro Copy / Tags** | `Manrope` | SemiBold | Tracking: +1.5% | Crisp, legible on small packaging tags |

### 3.1 Application Rules
1. **Headlines (`Unbounded`):** Used exclusively for menu category titles, outdoor signage, and hero promotional statements. Convey momentum and confidence.
2. **Body Text (`Manrope`):** Used for ingredient listings, nutritional facts, packaging instructions, and mobile app UI.

## 4. LOGO ARCHITECTURE & VECTOR GEOMETRY

### 4.1 Emblem Specification: "Dynamic Speed C"
The mark is a responsive vector monogram combining the letter **"C"** (representing *Chicken* and *Craft*) with a dynamic forward tilt.

* **Structural Composition:**
  1. **Layer 1 (Top Vector):** Stylized Flame / Green Leaf (Heat & Freshness).
  2. **Layer 2 (Middle Vector):** Crisp Layer Silhouette (Burger-Samsa / Baked Crust).
  3. **Layer 3 (Base Vector):** Semi-spherical Bowl / Plate (Generosity & Satisfaction).
  4. **Outer Envelope:** Enclosed within the curved silhouette of the monogram letter **"C"**.

### 4.2 Mathematical Rules & Clearances
* **Angle Axis:** `15.0°` forward right inclination (representing airport velocity, motion, and the 15-minute serving guarantee).
* **Corner Radii:** `4px` inner vector nodes, `8px` outer vector curves.
* **Negative Space:** The internal curve forms an organic drop silhouette (sauce/juiciness).
* **Clearance Zone (Safe Space):** `1.5X` where `X` equals the full width of the "C" emblem.
* **Minimum Scaling:**
  * Digital: `16 x 16 px` (App Icon / Favicon)
  * Print: `10 mm` width (Vector Lockup)

## 5. CULTURAL PATTERN SYSTEM: "NEO-GIRIH 15°"

To ground the brand in Samarkand's architectural legacy without appearing dated, traditional Uzbek *Girih* (interlocking star tile geometry) is re-engineered into a modern vector system aligned with the brand's 15° dynamic axis.

### 5.1 Construction & Vector Logic
* **Stroke Weight:** `1.5px` uniform line art.
* **Geometry:** Interlocking 8-pointed geometric stars angled along the `15.0°` tilt axis.
* **Color Application:** `Charcoal Black` at `4%` opacity on `Soft Canvas` backgrounds, or `Forest Leaf` tone-on-tone overlay.

### 5.2 Pattern Touchpoints
1. **Micro-Level (Isometric Icon Grid):** Fine line-art icons (bowl, chicken, timer, herb) printed on unbleached kraft paper for burger wrapping paper, coffee sleeves, and napkins.
2. **Macro-Level (15° Dynamic Stripes):** Bold `#FF5017` accent stripes cut at 15° for to-go packaging bags, cups, and menu board dividers.
3. **Hero-Level (Neo-Girih Vector Inlay):** Geometric star vector patterns used for acoustic wall panels in the restaurant interior, counter tile trims, and mobile app menu backgrounds.

## 6. IMAGERY & FOOD DIRECTION ("WARM NATURAL LIGHT")

Food presentation activates immediate hunger responses, eliminating any misconception of dry or sterile health food.

* **Lighting:** Single-source warm side sunlight with soft, organic shadows (simulating a bright Samarkand afternoon).
* **Focal Point:** Macro close-up shot focusing on crisp baked skin texture, glistened yogurt drizzle, and fresh herb garnishes.
* **Color Grading:** Warm undertones (+5 Golden saturation), true-to-life contrast, zero unnatural artificial filters.
* **Styling Rules:** Meals are always presented in deep, generous ceramic bowls or craft boxes filled with natural ingredients.

## 8. UI/UX & EDITORIAL LAYOUT PRINCIPLES

For digital menus, mobile ordering, and printed brand collateral:

* **Generous White Space:** Minimum `30%` unallocated space per screen/page to maintain readability and visual calm.
* **12-Column Responsive Grid:** `24px` gutters for desktop/tablet, `16px` gutters for mobile interfaces.
* **Component Radii:** Standard container corner radius set to `16px`, buttons set to `12px` or fully pill-shaped (`999px`).
* **Visual Anchor Accent:** Accent cards feature a `15°` diagonal corner clip on top-right borders for brand recognition.
