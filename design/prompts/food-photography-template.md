# ChickenFit Food Photography Prompt Template

Используйте этот шаблон для любого блюда. Каждый раз будет одинаковый стиль.

## Core Prompt (всегда в начале):
professional food photography, [полное описание блюда], warm directional lighting, high detail textures (crispy skin, juicy chicken, flaky dough layers), shallow depth of field, appetizing, cinematic composition, no background, transparent PNG style, consistent ChickenFit food photography style, clean edges

## Variables:
- [dish description] — например: "crispy chicken karaage with sesame seeds and fresh cilantro on wooden board"
- [special elements] — если нужно добавить (steam, sauce, etc.)

## Negative prompt (всегда в конце):
no background, no illustration, no cartoon, no text, no logo, no furniture, no people, no cold lighting, no over-saturated, no heavy styling, no steam too much

## Пример готового промпта:
professional food photography, crispy chicken karaage pieces with sesame seeds and fresh cilantro on wooden board, warm directional lighting, high detail textures, shallow depth of field, appetizing, cinematic composition, no background, transparent PNG style, consistent ChickenFit food photography style, clean edges

no background, no illustration, no cartoon, no text, no logo, no furniture, no people, no cold lighting, no over-saturated, no heavy styling
