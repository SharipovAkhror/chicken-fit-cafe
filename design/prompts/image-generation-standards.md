# Единый стиль живых фотографий ChickenFit

## Базовый системный промпт (копируй в каждый чат с любой моделью)

```
Brand: ChickenFit Cafe — warm, juicy, natural fast-casual chicken in flaky samsa-style pastry (UTP), Samarkand airport area.
Core product: chicken burger hybrid sealed in golden flaky layered pastry dough, visible cross-section when relevant, juicy chicken, fresh greens/sauce, steam, crumbs, oil sheen.
Core style (NEVER change):
- Warm, rich, appetizing food photography (Japanese flat-lay / studio food art hybrid)
- Colors: light pastry #F3E7D3 or #FAF7F0, terracotta #B94D2F, graphite #28211D, olive #667447
- Real food textures: crisp flaky pastry layers, juicy pulled/cut chicken meat fibers, fresh herbs, sesame seeds, glistening oil drops
- Lighting: warm directional natural studio light, crisp highlights on crust
- Background: pure clean light warm surface or isolated white/warm-white, high contrast
- Text: NO fake words, NO weird typography, pure imagery
```
